# Social engineering
- **Social engineering** is manipulation that deceives people into revealing confidential information.
- It can be used to gather sensitive information, gain unauthorised access, commit fraud, disrupt operations, or damage reputations.

### Techniques

| Technique | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Phishing | Pretending to be a legitimate organisation in a text, email, or call to gather information. |
| Pretexting | Creating a fictitious story or context to make a deceptive request seem believable. |
| Baiting | Offering an incentive to trick someone into revealing information or installing malware. |
| Quid pro quo | Persuading someone to provide information in exchange for something. |
| Scareware | Showing fake infection warnings to pressure users into downloading harmful software. |
| Shoulder surfing | Watching someone to steal information such as a PIN, password, or message. |
