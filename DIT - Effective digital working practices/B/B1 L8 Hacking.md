# Hacking
- **Hacking** means gaining unauthorised access to a computer system, network, or data.
- Hacking can be harmful, neutral, or beneficial, such as finding vulnerabilities so security can be improved.

### Types of hackers
- **White hat hackers** work to improve security by finding and fixing vulnerabilities.
- **Black hat hackers** exploit systems for personal gain, crime, or malicious purposes.
- **Grey hat hackers** explore systems and identify flaws, sometimes without permission.

### Ethical hacking and penetration testing
- **Ethical hacking** deliberately tests systems with permission to find weaknesses before malicious hackers exploit them.
- **Penetration testing** evaluates IT security by simulating a cyber attack using ethical hackers.
- It can identify weak points, reduce the risk of unauthorised access and data breaches, identify possible DoS exploits, and reveal vulnerabilities that malware could use.
- Testing must be authorised, follow agreed rules, and aim to identify and fix weaknesses. Ethical hackers should follow laws and professional codes of conduct.
