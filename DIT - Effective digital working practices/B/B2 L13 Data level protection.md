# Data level protection
- **Data level protection** focuses on protecting the data itself, rather than only the systems, devices, or networks that store or transmit it.
- Even if someone gains access to a system, protected data should remain unreadable unless they are authorised.

### Methods
- **Encryption:** Converts data into unreadable form unless the correct decryption key is available. Protect stored and transmitted data.
- Firewalls (hardware and software) filter traffic and help prevent unauthorised access.
- Anti-virus software detects, blocks, and removes malicious software.
- **Device hardening** reduces vulnerabilities and removes unnecessary features that attackers could exploit.
- Software and interface design can protect data through choices such as obscuring data entry, autocomplete, or a “stay logged in” option.
- Back-up and recovery procedures help restore data after loss or an incident.

### Why it matters
- Protects sensitive and personal data.
- Helps organisations comply with laws such as GDPR.
- Reduces the damage caused by data breaches.
- Supports secure data sharing across systems and devices.
