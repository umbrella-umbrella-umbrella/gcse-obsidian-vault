# Impact of modern technologies
- Modern technologies include communication tools such as Microsoft Teams and Zoom, devices such as smartphones and desktops, and local or web-based platforms such as Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.
- Their use can affect both individuals and organisations.

### Areas of impact

| Individuals | Organisations |
| --- | --- |
| Flexible and remote working can change working styles. | Infrastructure needs to support the technology. |
| Technology can affect mental health. | Security and distribution of data need consideration. |
| Inclusive and accessible technology can help more people participate. | Technology can support remote working and collaboration. |
|  | Organisations must consider legislation and accessibility standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), described in the source as an international benchmark for accessible digital content. |
