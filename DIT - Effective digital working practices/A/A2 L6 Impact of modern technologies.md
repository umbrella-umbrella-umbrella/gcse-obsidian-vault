# Impact of modern technologies
- Modern technologies include communication tools such as Microsoft Teams and Zoom, devices such as smartphones and desktops, and local or web-based platforms such as Google Workspace and Microsoft 365.
- Their use can affect both individuals and organisations.

### Impact on individuals
- Flexible and remote working can change working styles.
- Technology can affect mental health.
- Inclusive and accessible technology can help more people participate.

### Impact on organisations
- Organisations need suitable infrastructure and must consider security and data distribution.
- Modern technology can support remote working and collaboration.
- Organisations must consider relevant legislation when designing or choosing platforms.
- Accessible platforms help organisations meet accessibility standards such as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG), described in the source as an international benchmark for accessible digital content.
