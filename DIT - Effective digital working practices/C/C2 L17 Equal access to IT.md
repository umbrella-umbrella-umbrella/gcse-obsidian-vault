# Equal access to IT
- **Equal access to IT** means everyone has a fair and reasonable opportunity to use digital technology, online services, and information systems.
- When people are excluded, a **digital divide** is created: the gap between people with access to digital technologies, fast internet, and digital skills and those without them.

### Net neutrality
- **Net neutrality** is the principle that internet service providers (ISPs) should treat all internet traffic equally.
- Websites, apps, and online services should have the same priority and access speed; users, rather than providers, decide what content to access.

### Benefits of equal access

| Individuals | Organisations | Society |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Access to online education, remote work, employment opportunities, healthcare, and banking. | A larger talent pool, better productivity and customer communication, and more innovation and creativity. | Reduced inequality, economic growth, improved education, and more connected communities. |

### Legislation and standards

| Law or standard | Contribution to equal access |
| --- | --- |
| Equality Act | Protects against discrimination and supports fair access to services, employment, and digital systems, including reasonable adjustments. |
| Digital Economy Act | Supports digital inclusion through access to affordable broadband and modern communications services. |
| World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) | Publishes internationally recognised accessibility standards. |
