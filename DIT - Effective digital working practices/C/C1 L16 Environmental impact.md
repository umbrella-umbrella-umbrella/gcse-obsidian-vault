# Environmental impact
- The manufacturing, use, and disposal of IT systems have significant environmental impacts.

### Environmental factors

| Factor | Description |
| --- | --- |
| E-waste | Discarded electronic devices such as smartphones, computers, and televisions. |
| Energy consumption | Digital devices and data centres use significant amounts of electricity. |
| Resource depletion | Manufacturing devices requires raw materials, including rare earth metals. |
| Planned obsolescence | Rapid technological change can shorten the useful life of devices. |
| Digital downloads | Obtaining content from the internet and storing it locally; the source notes savings in packaging. |
| Green IT | Environmentally sustainable computing practices, such as using low-power mode on smartphones. |

### Managing environmental impact
- Before replacing or upgrading a device, consider whether the current system fails to meet performance needs, whether the new system can integrate with existing systems, and whether it improves data security and privacy.
