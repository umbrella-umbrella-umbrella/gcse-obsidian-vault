# User access restriction
- **User access restriction** limits what users can see, do, or change in a system, based on their identity and what they need to do.
- Access levels are usually assigned according to roles, such as administrator, teacher, student, or employee. This helps protect data and systems.

### Methods
- **Physical security:** Locks, key cards, and barriers prevent unauthorised physical access to buildings, rooms, or devices.
- **Biometrics:** Uses unique physical or behavioural characteristics to identify or verify a person.
- **User access levels:** Give users permissions appropriate to their roles.
- **Passwords:** Secret words or phrases verify identity before access is allowed.
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA):** Requires two different types of verification.
- **Secure backups:** Safely stored copies help recover data after loss, corruption, or an attack.

### Strong passwords
- Use at least 12 characters, with a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Avoid common words and personal information.
- Change passwords regularly and do not reuse them across accounts.
