
## **Extra content added**

**Characteristics and interdependence:** Tropical rainforests are hot and wet throughout the year, with layered vegetation and very high biodiversity. Climate and water availability support rapid plant growth. Plants provide food and habitats for animals; decomposers recycle nutrients, which are quickly taken up by vegetation. People depend on rainforests for food, materials, medicines, jobs and other resources. Biodiversity is threatened when habitats are cleared or fragmented.

**Deforestation:** Rates and locations vary over time. Alongside farming, road building and settlement, causes include logging, mineral extraction, energy development and population growth. Impacts include economic gains and employment, but also soil erosion, habitat and biodiversity loss, and increased carbon dioxide emissions.

**Sustainable management:** Approaches include selective logging and replanting, conservation and education, ecotourism, international agreements controlling tropical hardwood use, and debt reduction. These aim to preserve biodiversity and livelihoods while allowing sustainable use of forest resources.
