# 4.4 Bioenergetics


> AQA GCSE Biology 8461 | Topics 4.1-4.4 | Source: supplied 2016 specification

## Photosynthesis


Photosynthesis is an endothermic reaction: energy is transferred from the environment by light and absorbed by chloroplasts.

Carbon dioxide + water --light--> glucose + oxygen

Symbol equation: 6CO2 + 6H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. Recognise CO2, H2O, O2 and C6H12O6.

### Rate and limiting factors


Rate is affected by light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, temperature and amount of chlorophyll. Increasing one factor raises rate only until another factor becomes limiting. HT: factors interact, so any one may limit; explain graphs with two or three factors and identify the limiting factor. In greenhouses, compare the yield benefit of extra heat, light or carbon dioxide with its cost to maximise profit.

Rate = amount of oxygen produced (or carbon dioxide used) / time. For an inverse-square relationship, light intensity is proportional to 1 / distance squared. Doubling distance reduces intensity to one quarter; halving distance makes it four times greater. HT: use inverse proportion and inverse square law in this context.

### Required practical 6: light intensity and photosynthesis


1. Place pondweed (or another aquatic organism) in water with the cut end positioned to release gas; allow it to adjust to the light.
2. Change light intensity by moving a lamp to measured distances. Keep temperature, carbon dioxide availability, organism length/species and measurement time constant.
3. Measure oxygen production over a fixed time, for example bubbles per minute or gas volume collected. Repeat at each distance and calculate means.
4. Plot rate against light intensity (or distance, as directed). Identify where another factor may limit the rate.
Bubble counts are an estimate because bubbles vary in size. Gas-volume measurement is more direct. Prevent heating by the lamp; control other variables and allow equilibration.

### Uses of glucose from photosynthesis


- Respiration.
- Converted to starch for storage.
- Made into fats or oils for storage.
- Made into cellulose for cell walls.
- Combined with nitrate ions from soil to make amino acids, then proteins.

## Respiration


Respiration is an exothermic reaction that happens continuously in living cells. It transfers energy needed for life processes, including building larger molecules, movement and keeping warm.

Aerobic equation: glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water. Symbol equation: C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O.

Anaerobic respiration in muscles: glucose -> lactic acid. It incompletely oxidises glucose and transfers much less energy than aerobic respiration. In plants and yeast: glucose -> ethanol + carbon dioxide. In yeast this is fermentation, used in making bread and alcoholic drinks.

### Exercise and oxygen debt


During exercise, heart rate, breathing rate and breath volume increase to deliver more oxygenated blood to muscles. If oxygen supply is insufficient, muscles respire anaerobically. Lactic acid accumulates, contributing to fatigue; during prolonged vigorous activity muscles may stop contracting efficiently. HT: blood carries lactic acid to the liver, where it is converted back to glucose. Oxygen debt is the extra oxygen needed after exercise to react with accumulated lactic acid and remove it from cells.

## Metabolism


Metabolism is the sum of all the reactions in a cell or the body. Energy transferred by respiration powers enzyme-controlled reactions that synthesise and break down molecules.

- Glucose can be converted to starch, glycogen or cellulose.
- Lipids are formed from glycerol and three fatty acid molecules.
- Glucose and nitrate ions are used to form amino acids, which are used to make proteins.
- Respiration is part of metabolism.
- Excess proteins are broken down to form urea for excretion.

## Equations and calculations quick reference


- Magnification = image size / real size.
- Percentage change = (final - initial) / initial x 100.
- Rate = amount of change / time; for a fixed endpoint, rate is often compared as 1 / time.
- Area of colony or clear zone = pi x r squared.
- Binary fission population = starting number x 2^(number of divisions); divisions = elapsed time / mean division time.
- Surface area to volume ratio = surface area / volume.
- Light intensity is proportional to 1 / distance squared (HT).
- Photosynthesis: carbon dioxide + water -> glucose + oxygen (light).
- Aerobic respiration: glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water.
- Anaerobic respiration: glucose -> lactic acid (muscles); glucose -> ethanol + carbon dioxide (plants/yeast).

## High-value exam distinctions


- Diffusion moves particles down a concentration gradient; osmosis is specifically water crossing a partially permeable membrane; active transport moves substances against a concentration gradient using energy from respiration.
- Respiration is not breathing: respiration is a chemical reaction in cells that transfers energy.
- Antibiotics treat bacterial infections, not viral infections; painkillers relieve symptoms but do not kill pathogens.
- Vaccination stimulates antibody production before infection; antibodies are specific to antigens.
- Benign tumours remain in place; malignant tumours invade and can spread to form secondary tumours.
- Xylem carries water and mineral ions from roots; phloem translocates dissolved sugars from leaves to other parts.
- Photosynthesis is endothermic; respiration is exothermic.
- Once another factor limits a process, increasing the original factor has little or no further effect.
